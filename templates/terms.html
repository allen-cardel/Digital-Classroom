Terms of Use and Privacy Notice

Interactive Digital Classroom

1. Access

Use this portal for legitimate classroom purposes. Keep your password confidential and do not attempt to access another person’s account or data.

You are responsible for activity under your account. Notify an administrator if you believe your login details have been compromised.

For account access or privacy concerns, contact your school administrator.
